WHO WE ARE

Helping young people turn potential into progress.

Youth's Network And Skills Impact is a youth-focused network designed to make opportunities, practical skills and useful connections easier to access.

We aim to support young people through opportunity sharing, training, collaboration and access to information that can help them make informed decisions about their future.

Our Mission

Connect young people to useful opportunities, skills development and networks that support personal and professional growth.

02

Our Vision

A future where young people across Africa can discover opportunities and build the confidence and skills to pursue them.

03

Our Values

Integrity, inclusion, practical learning, collaboration, accountability and respect for every member.
